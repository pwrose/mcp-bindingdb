👤 User

Start from sildenafil: find its close analogues in BindingDB and tell me which targets besides PDE5 they hit.

🧠 Assistant

Short answer: sildenafil (BDBM14390) has 35 close analogues in BindingDB (Tanimoto >= 0.7) that have measurements. Together they bind 40 targets at 10 uM or better. Apart from PDE5 itself, the hits fall into three groups, and only the first group reflects sildenafil's own activity.

1. Other phosphodiesterases (a known class effect). Sildenafil itself was measured against these, so the activity belongs to the drug:

Off-target Best sildenafil value Comment
PDE6 (rod/cone photoreceptor subunits and complexes) IC50 9.5-33 nM ~10x weaker than PDE5 (IC50 1 nM, 71 measurements); the reason for the blue-tinted vision side effect
PDE1A / 1B / 1C IC50 260 nM - 1.5 uM
PDE11A, PDE10A, PDE9A IC50 2.3-3.4 uM weak

2. Histone deacetylases (HDAC1, 2, 3, 6), seen only in designed analogues. These hits come from a 2016 J. Med. Chem. series (PMID 27606546). In that series a hydroxamic acid, a classic zinc-binding HDAC warhead, was added to the sildenafil piperazine to make dual PDE5/HDAC inhibitors. Sildenafil has no measured HDAC activity in BindingDB. How much HDAC activity an analogue has depends on the linker:

3. Single measurements of sildenafil. Adenosine A2a receptor (Ki 200 nM, one measurement), ABCC5/MRP5 transporter (Ki 1.2 uM), and the hERG channel KCNH2 (IC50 3.3 uM). Each of these rests on a single paper and needs confirming before you read anything into it.

Many "PDE5" rows in the target summary are the same enzyme in another species (rat, dog, cow) or a truncated construct (e.g. O76074[531-875]). They are not separate off-targets.

Lesson: an analogue's target is not the drug's target. Searching by similarity and then summarizing by target is a fast way to generate repurposing or off-target hypotheses, but it pools many compounds. Before you attribute a target to sildenafil, check which compound carries the measurement. The HDAC hits above look striking in the summary, yet they come from analogues that were engineered to hit HDACs. The PDE6 and PDE1 hits, by contrast, are measured on sildenafil itself and match its known side-effect profile.

How this was answered

  1. chemical_name_to_smiles ("sildenafil"): resolved the name to BDBM14390 and its SMILES.
  2. similarity_search (threshold 0.7, summarize_by_target=true, max_value_nm=10000): grouped all close analogues by the targets they bind, giving 35 compounds and 40 targets.
  3. similarity_search (same threshold, no summary): listed the analogues themselves, which showed the hydroxamic-acid series.
  4. find_targets_for_compound for sildenafil (max_value_nm=10000) and for two hydroxamate analogues: checked which compound each off-target activity belongs to.
