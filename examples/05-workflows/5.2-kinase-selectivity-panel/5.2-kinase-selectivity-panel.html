🧠 Assistant

Choosing the panel. I restricted the data to Kd values on wild-type human proteins, which drops mutants and truncated constructs such as P00519[201-500,M351T]. On that basis 371 kinases were measured for all four drugs. Most of them are bound by none of the four, so the heat map shows the 48 kinases where at least one drug reaches median pKd >= 8 (Kd <= 10 nM). The figure below shows the result, with columns clustered by profile.

  • Imatinib and nilotinib are the selective pair. Both bind the same small set: ABL1/ABL2, KIT, PDGFRA/B, CSF1R, DDR1/DDR2 and LCK (the leftmost block). They show no detectable binding (hatched cells) to most of the rest.
  • Dasatinib is the broadest. It binds the whole SRC family (SRC, LCK, LYN, FYN, YES1, HCK, FGR, BLK, FRK), the Ephrin receptors, the TEC kinases BTK, BMX and TXK, and the imatinib targets as well. That breadth is why it has more off-target effects than imatinib.
  • Bosutinib is broad in a different way. It is the most potent of the four on ABL1 (median pKd 10.2) and SRC (9.0), but it spares KIT and PDGFRA/B (pKd 5.3-6.7). It hits a group of STE20/MAP4K kinases (MAP4K2-5, MINK1, SLK, STK10) and MAP2K2, which the other three barely touch. The clustering pulls these into their own block.

Lesson: check where a heat map's numbers come from. About 2,370 of the Kd values here come from just two kinome-wide screens: Davis et al. 2011 (Nat. Biotechnol., doi 10.1038/nbt.1990, 1,744 values) and Karaman et al. 2008 (doi 10.1038/nbt1358, 628 values). For nilotinib and bosutinib, most cells rest on a single measurement. Imatinib and dasatinib have up to 35 measurements per kinase. A broad, dense matrix like this usually means a few large panels, not many independent studies, so treat small differences (< 0.5 log units) as noise. Two choices also shape the picture: mixing Kd with IC50 values would change it, because IC50 depends on assay conditions; and "> 10 uM" values are not real numbers, which is why they are hatched rather than coloured.

How this was answered

  1. run_sql on compound and compound_name: found each drug's BindingDB record(s) and InChIKey.
  2. run_sql on activity: kept Kd values on wild-type human targets (uniprot_raw = uniprot, organism = 'Homo sapiens'), took the median pKd per drug and kinase, kept kinases measured for all four drugs, then those where one drug reaches pKd >= 8. A second query counted measurements per source paper.
  3. matplotlib (outside the server): drew the heat map, ordering the columns by average-linkage clustering of the profiles and hatching cells where all values were censored ("> 10 uM").
  4. add_figure: added the image so the transcript could include it. The matrix is also saved as heatmap_matrix_pKd.csv next to this transcript.
