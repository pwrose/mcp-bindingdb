👤 User

Find close analogues of imatinib (Tanimoto ≥ 0.7) that bind any target below 100 nM.

🧠 Assistant

Out of 1,464,874 compounds, 83 have a Tanimoto similarity of 0.7 or more to imatinib. 65 of them have at least one Ki, Kd, IC50 or EC50 measurement at or below 100 nM.

The 83 include imatinib itself under three records (similarity 1.0) and two deuterated copies (0.90). The rest fall into three families:

  • Patent series around the pyridine ring. Most of the potent hits come from two patents, US11725005 and US10906896, which add a second (hetero)aryl ring to imatinib's pyridine. They are measured mainly on KIT, at 4–20 nM.
  • Classic medicinal-chemistry analogues from journal papers: halogens on the benzamide ring, a methyl on the pyridine, or a missing methyl.
  • Other drugs. Masitinib (similarity 0.75) replaces imatinib's pyrimidine with a thiazole. It is a separate approved drug with 452 measurements, and it binds ABL1 at Kd 2.1 nM.

Lesson: what a similarity number means. Similarity here is the Tanimoto coefficient on Morgan fingerprints (radius 2, 2048 bits): the share of small structural fragments two molecules have in common. Some rough guides:

  • 1.0: the same structure, ignoring stereo, salts and isotopes. That is why imatinib appears three times.
  • ≥ 0.7: close analogues, typically one or two changes to the same molecule.
  • 0.4–0.6: often the same chemical series, with larger changes.

A small change can still shift the score a lot. Nilotinib, a second-generation drug built on the same 2-anilino-4-(3-pyridyl)pyrimidine core, scores only about 0.52 against imatinib, so this search misses it. To find every compound with a given core, use a substructure search (Example 2.5). Also note what the affinity filter does: "best measurement ≤ 100 nM" says only that the compound hit something that strongly, and each compound was measured on its own small panel of targets. The values are not comparable across compounds unless they come from the same assay.

How this was answered

  1. similarity_search with imatinib's SMILES, threshold=0.7: 83 similar compounds across the whole database.
  2. similarity_search with the same query and max_value_nm=100: 65 compounds, each with its most potent qualifying measurement. With an affinity filter, only the 747,035 compounds that have measurements are searched.
  3. find_targets_for_compound for masitinib with max_value_nm=100: its strongest targets.
  4. The nilotinib–imatinib similarity was computed separately with RDKit, using the same fingerprint settings.
  5. draw_structure_grid: draws the selected analogues aligned on their shared core.
