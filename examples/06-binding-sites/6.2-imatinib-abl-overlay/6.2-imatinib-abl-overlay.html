How the four were picked. I searched ABL1 (P00519, polymerid 5221) for ligands with PDB complexes, then kept the approved drugs that have both a co-crystal and a Ki/Kd measurement. That gives imatinib plus nilotinib, dasatinib and bosutinib. Two notable exclusions: ponatinib is structurally the next-closest analog and has 3IK3/3OXZ, but BindingDB only carries IC50 for it; asciminib has a Kd and 5MO4/8SSN, but it binds the myristoyl pocket, not the ATP site, so it would not overlay meaningfully. By Morgan fingerprint, nilotinib is the only true close analog of imatinib (Tanimoto 0.71) — dasatinib and bosutinib are separate chemotypes hitting the same pocket, which is what makes the overlay interesting.

What the overlay shows. Four entries superposed on 3K5V (imatinib, 1.74 Å) with pocket RMSDs of 0.44–0.61 Å, so the fits are clean.

  • All four hydrogen bond to the hinge methionine M337 — the one invariant anchor.
  • The two type II ligands, imatinib and nilotinib, thread past the gatekeeper into the back pocket and pick up the DFG-out signature: H-bonds to E305 (αC glutamate) and D400 (DFG aspartate). Imatinib adds contacts to T334, I379 and H380 that nilotinib's trifluoromethyl-imidazole arm does not make.
  • Dasatinib and bosutinib stop at the adenine pocket (M337 + gatekeeper T334) and make no back-pocket H-bonds. Their DFG motif is in the active conformation: D400/F401/G402 sit 4–6 Å from their imatinib-bound positions, and the activation loop downstream (L403, L406) moves 13–26 Å. Bosutinib's entry also shows a displaced P-loop (G268–Y272 shifted 4–8 Å) folding over its dichloro-methoxyaniline.

On the affinities — the comparison only works pairwise. Imatinib (Ki 25 nM, median of 6) and nilotinib (Ki 1 nM) are both Ki, so nilotinib's ~25-fold gain is a real like-for-like comparison. Bosutinib (Kd 0.062 nM, median of 15) and dasatinib (Kd 0.53 nM) are both Kd. Ki and Kd come from different assay formats and are not interchangeable, so the apparent 400-fold gap between imatinib and bosutinib is an artifact of mixing types, not a measured difference. The viewer's Affinity menu lets you switch all four bars to a single type if you want a consistent axis — IC50 is the one type all four share.
