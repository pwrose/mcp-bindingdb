Lesson: every number has a provenance, so open it before you trust it. BindingDB records come from two routes. Some are curated directly from articles and patents, and these often carry pH, temperature, enzyme and substrate concentrations and a detailed protocol. Others are imported from ChEMBL and usually carry a one-line assay description. Across the whole database, only about 213,000 of 3.25 million measurements (6.5%) report a pH. IC50 values depend on the assay format and on ATP and enzyme concentrations, which explains why gefitinib's EGFR values span six orders of magnitude (pAffinity 4.0–10.0). The "best" value of 0.1 nM is about 140-fold more potent than the median. Use get_activity to see where a surprising number came from, and cite the original paper, not just the database.
