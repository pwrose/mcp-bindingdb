In BindingDB, EGFR mutants are separate targets that share the UniProt accession P00533 and carry the mutation in brackets. Searching for "EGFR" in human returns wild-type EGFR plus more than a dozen variants. The two compared here:

Other variants hold more data than T790M alone. The double mutant [T790M, L858R] (target 60) has 1,276 compounds, and [L858R, T790M, C797S] (target 1058) has 666. T790M is the "gatekeeper" resistance mutation that lets lung tumours escape first-generation drugs such as gefitinib and erlotinib.

A fair comparison. I kept only exact ("=") Ki, Kd or IC50 values and compared each compound's wild-type and T790M values within the same source (same paper or patent, same affinity type). This gives 61 compounds (64 compound–source pairs). Of these, 26 pairs are at least 10-fold more potent on T790M, 4 are at least 10-fold more potent on wild type, and the median shift is +0.4 log units toward the mutant.

Two chemotypes separate cleanly. The 2,4-dianilinopyrimidines with an acrylamide (rociletinib and its patent analogues) are designed to be mutant-selective, and they are 100-fold or more more potent on T790M. The 4-anilinoquinazolines (gefitinib and the US9556191B2 series) lose potency on the mutant. That is the drug-resistance mechanism, visible directly in the data.

Lesson: compare like with like. Gefitinib appears twice, and the two patents disagree: 135-fold weaker on T790M in one, only 3.5-fold weaker in the other. Assay format, ATP concentration and enzyme construct all shift IC50 values. Comparing a wild-type value from one paper with a mutant value from another can produce or hide a selectivity window. Pair measurements by source, as done here, before drawing conclusions. Also note that find_ligands_for_target with include_variants=true pools all EGFR variants into one ranked list. That is useful for a quick overview, but to compare variants, query each variant's target id separately.

How this was answered

  1. search_targets with query "EGFR", organism "Homo sapiens": listed wild-type EGFR (id 520) and its mutant and truncated constructs, including T790M (id 243).
  2. find_ligands_for_target with uniprot P00533, include_variants true: a pooled ranking across all EGFR variants. Wild-type measurements dominate the top of the list.
  3. run_sql on the activity table: paired each compound's median pAffinity on targets 520 and 243 within the same source and affinity type, then joined to compound for names and SMILES.
