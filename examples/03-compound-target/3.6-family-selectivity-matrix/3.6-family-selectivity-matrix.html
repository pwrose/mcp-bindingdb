One series dominates the JAK1-selective end of BindingDB.

The four family members resolve to JAK1 P23458 (9,772 compounds), JAK2 O60674 (12,326), JAK3 P52333 (8,396) and TYK2 P29597 (4,491). Taking JAK1 as the primary target leaves 4,624 compounds with an exact IC50 of 50 nM or better at JAK1 and at least one measurement elsewhere in the family.

Values are IC50 as p_affinity (9 − log10 nM), so 9.77 is 0.17 nM.

  • The top of the list is a single chemotype: 5-fluoro-2-(arylamino)pyrimidin-4-yl indoles carrying an (R)-3-methoxy-2-(4-methylpiperazin-1-yl)propanamide on the indole. BDBM801878 is the best of them, 0.17 nM at JAK1 and 2,754-fold over its nearest relative.
  • JAK2 is always the kinase that limits selectivity — it is the most potent off-target for every row here, at IC50 468–100 nM.
  • JAK3 never binds these compounds measurably. Its column is 5.0 throughout, which is an upper bound from > 10 µM values, so JAK3 is listed in each row's bounds. It does not limit the reported selectivity, because JAK2 is weaker still.
  • One row is not comparable to the rest: BDBM50031738 ranks third at ≥1,905× but was measured against only 2 of the four kinases, and its JAK2 value is itself a > bound. Its selectivity is a lower bound on thin evidence, not a measured ratio.

These are IC50 values from different papers, so the ratios are a guide to rank rather than to absolute fold-selectivity.

🧠 Assistant

Requiring all four measurements keeps the series and drops the outlier.

With require_all_targets, 1,456 compounds qualify and the top six are the same series as before — BDBM50031738 is the only compound to fall out of the leading group, which is the one whose JAK2 datapoint was a > bound.

The structures show why the ranking is so flat across the six: they differ only in the group on the aminopyrimidine's aryl ring (methyl ester, oxazole, benzothiazole, ethyl ester, glycolate), while the indole and the methoxy-methylpiperazinyl-propanamide are constant. Selectivity moves between 1,413× and 2,754× across that whole set, so none of those substituents is what buys JAK1 selectivity; the shared core is.

For a JAK1-selective starting point this series is the obvious anchor, with the caveat that its selectivity over JAK2 — the only family member it actually binds — is about 3 log units and no better.
