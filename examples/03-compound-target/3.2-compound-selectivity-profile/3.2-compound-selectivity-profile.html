Which human targets does imatinib bind below 100 nM?

🧠 Assistant

Asked for "targets below 100 nM", BindingDB returns 35 target records where imatinib (BDBM13530) has at least one Ki, Kd, IC50 or EC50 ≤ 100 nM. Only 16 of those are distinct human wild-type proteins:

Kind of target record Count Examples
Human wild-type protein 16 ABL1, KIT, PDGFR-alpha/beta, DDR1, CSF1R, LCK
Mutant or truncated construct of a human protein 12 ABL1 [201-500, E255K], ABL1 [229-512], KIT [V559D], DDR2 [A642S]
Non-human protein 4 mouse ABL1, mouse PDGFR-beta
Protein complex 3 BCR-ABL fusion, ABL1/ABL2, PDGFR-alpha/beta

BindingDB stores each mutant, kinase-domain construct and species as its own target. A selectivity profile therefore looks longer than the list of distinct proteins: ABL1 alone appears as 9 records in addition to wild-type ABL1.

The 16 human wild-type targets are in the grid below, one bar per target. Each bar is the median of one measurement type, Kd wherever imatinib has Kd values on that target, because mixing Kd with cellular IC50s in one median would make the targets incomparable. The note under each bar gives the number of values, the best one, and the median of the other types. The three carbonic anhydrases share one bar, and the last three bars are single measurements.

The well-supported targets are the expected ones: ABL1 and ABL2, KIT, PDGFR-alpha and -beta, DDR1 and DDR2, CSF1R and LCK, all with Kd medians between 0.7 and 43 nM. Imatinib also hits non-kinases: NQO2 and the drug transporter MATE1 (three IC50s each, medians 82 and 50 nM), and carbonic anhydrases 1, 2 and 9, whose values are single Ki measurements from one 2009 paper.

The measurement type matters as much as the target. On the same kinase, imatinib's IC50 median is 2- to 500-fold weaker than its Kd median: ABL1 has a Kd median of 35 nM over 32 values but an IC50 median of 300 nM over 39, and DDR1 goes from 0.7 nM (Kd) to 337 nM (IC50). Taken over all types together, as a plain median of pAffinity, ABL1 comes out at only 6.8 (about 160 nM) across 85 measurements, and KIT at 7.0 across 52.

Lesson: a threshold filter tests the best value, not the typical one. max_value_nm = 100 keeps a target if any one measurement passes. EGFR and ERBB2 make the list from one sub-nanomolar IC50 each, from one 2022 paper; they are the faint bars at the bottom of the grid. EGFR's 25 measurements have a median of 10 µM, and ERBB2's two Kd values are both above 10 µM, so imatinib does not meaningfully inhibit either. ABL1 is the opposite case: it is imatinib's main target, yet only 38 of its 85 values are ≤ 100 nM, because cellular IC50s run weaker than biochemical Kd and Ki. Before you call something a target, check how many measurements support it, of which type, and what their median is.

How this was answered

  • find_targets_for_compound with monomerid 13530 (imatinib, found in Example 1.1), max_value_nm 100: every target with at least one measurement ≤ 100 nM, one row per target with its best value.
  • run_sql on the activity table: for the human wild-type targets (UniProt accession without mutation brackets), the number of measurements, the median pAffinity and the count ≤ 100 nM; then the same broken down by affinity type, with the median of the exact ('=') values of each type.
  • draw_structure_grid: one card for imatinib with a bar per target, using the Kd median where there is one, faint bars for targets with one or two measurements.
