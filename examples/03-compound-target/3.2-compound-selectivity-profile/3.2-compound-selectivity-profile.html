Which human targets does imatinib bind below 100 nM?

🧠 Assistant

Asked for "targets below 100 nM", BindingDB returns 35 target records where imatinib (BDBM13530) has at least one Ki, Kd, IC50 or EC50 ≤ 100 nM. Only 16 of those are distinct human wild-type proteins:

Kind of target record Count Examples
Human wild-type protein 16 ABL1, KIT, PDGFR-alpha/beta, DDR1, CSF1R, LCK
Mutant or truncated construct of a human protein 12 ABL1 [201-500, E255K], ABL1 [229-512], KIT [V559D], DDR2 [A642S]
Non-human protein 4 mouse ABL1, mouse PDGFR-beta
Protein complex 3 BCR-ABL fusion, ABL1/ABL2, PDGFR-alpha/beta

BindingDB stores each mutant, kinase-domain construct and species as its own target. A selectivity profile therefore looks longer than the list of distinct proteins: ABL1 alone appears as 9 records in addition to wild-type ABL1.

The well-supported targets are the expected ones: ABL1/ABL2, KIT, PDGFR-alpha/beta, DDR1/2, CSF1R and LCK. Imatinib also hits several non-kinases: NQO2, the drug transporter MATE1, and carbonic anhydrases. The carbonic anhydrase values are single measurements from one paper.

Lesson: a threshold filter tests the best value, not the typical one. max_value_nm = 100 keeps a target if any one measurement passes. EGFR and ERBB2 make the list from one sub-nanomolar IC50 each, from one 2022 paper. EGFR's other 24 measurements give a median of 5.0 (10 µM), so imatinib does not meaningfully inhibit EGFR. ABL1 is the opposite case: it is imatinib's main target, but its median is only 6.8. Its 85 values mix biochemical Kd/Ki with cellular IC50s, which run weaker. Before you call something a target, check how many measurements support it and what their median is.

How this was answered

  1. find_targets_for_compound with monomerid 13530 (imatinib, found in Example 1.1), max_value_nm 100: every target with at least one measurement ≤ 100 nM, one row per target with its best value.
  2. run_sql on the activity table: for human wild-type targets (UniProt accession without mutation brackets), computed the number of measurements, the median and the count ≤ 100 nM.
