What are the 20 most potent EGFR inhibitors in BindingDB with a Ki or Kd below 1 nM?

🧠 Assistant

BindingDB has 14,126 compounds measured against human EGFR (wild type, UniProt P00533; BindingDB target id 520). Restricting to Ki or Kd ≤ 1 nM still leaves more than 20 compounds. The grid below shows the 15 most potent, ranked by each compound's single best Ki or Kd, with its structure and a bar for that value. Where a compound has several values of the same type, a second bar gives their median; faint bars are compounds with a single Ki/Kd value on EGFR.

The top of the list: PD153035 (Ki 0.006 nM), CHEMBL4100860 (Ki 0.07 nM), then canertinib and afatinib (both Kd 0.1 nM) and erlotinib (Ki 0.1 nM). Pelitinib, neratinib and gefitinib follow at 0.23–0.4 nM. Three compounds tie at 0.35 nM: staurosporine, CHEMBL4098072 and CHEMBL5274166.

Ranks 16–20 are single-measurement compounds from medicinal-chemistry papers, plus a second BindingDB record for gefitinib (BDBM50338602, same InChIKey as BDBM5447) with one Ki of 0.4 nM. The same drug can sit under two BDBM ids, so a top-N list can count it twice.

Most of the list is the 4-anilinoquinazoline chemotype, highlighted in the grid: PD153035, gefitinib, erlotinib, canertinib, afatinib and two single-paper analogues. Pelitinib and neratinib carry the closely related 3-cyanoquinoline, and one compound is the quinoline version of the erlotinib core. Eight compounds also carry an acrylamide or crotonamide, the warhead of covalent EGFR inhibitors: canertinib, afatinib, pelitinib, neratinib, the quinazoline CHEMBL3883534, the anilinopyrimidine CHEMBL1229592 and the two anilinopyridines CHEMBL4100860 and CHEMBL4098072.

Lesson: a top-N list ranks single best values. The tool ranks each compound by its one most potent measurement, so a compound measured once in one paper can top a drug measured 40 times. PD153035 is first because of a single Ki of 6 pM. The tool also returns relations: CHEMBL5394686 is listed as "< 0.2 nM", which is an upper bound, not an exact value. Several compounds show how much a single value can overstate potency; the gap between their two bars is the point. Staurosporine has a best Kd of 0.35 nM, but its median over 25 Kd values is about 270 nM (pAffinity 6.6). CHEMBL4098072 has a best Ki of 0.35 nM and a median of 100 nM over 6 Ki values. CHEMBL1229592 has a best of 0.3 nM and a median of 14 nM (pAffinity 7.85). Read the number of measurements and the median before you believe a ranking.

How this was answered

  • search_targets with query "EGFR", organism "Homo sapiens": found wild-type EGFR (target id 520, 14,126 compounds). It also listed mutant and truncated EGFR constructs as separate targets (see Example 3.3).
  • find_ligands_for_target with target_id 520, affinity_types [Ki, Kd], max_value_nm 1, limit 20: the most potent compounds, one row per compound.
  • run_sql on the activity and compound tables: added SMILES, the number of Ki and Kd measurements, and the median of each type for each compound, and checked that the two gefitinib records share an InChIKey.
  • draw_structure_grid: the top 15 compounds with their best and median values as bars, aligned on the 4-anilinoquinazoline/quinoline core.
