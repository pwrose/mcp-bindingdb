👤 User

Find the human EGFR protein in BindingDB. How much data is there?

🧠 Assistant

Human EGFR (epidermal growth factor receptor, UniProt P00533) is BindingDB target 520, with 23,266 measurements on 14,126 compounds. Most of these are IC50 values:

Affinity type Measurements Compounds Best pAffinity
IC50 20,997 13,813 11.7
Kd 1,593 180 10.0
Ki 409 288 11.22
EC50 267 147 9.0

Other facts from the target record: 1,210 residues, ChEMBL id CHEMBL1955, 278 linked PDB structures (for example 1M17, erlotinib bound to the kinase domain), and synonyms including ERBB1, HER1 and EGFR_HUMAN.

One gene, many BindingDB targets. Target 520 is only the wild-type record. BindingDB stores every mutant and construct as a separate target, and P00533 has 56 of them, holding 31,607 measurements in total. The largest are:

The variant is written in the target name and in uniprot_raw: P00533[T790M,L858R] is a double mutant, and P00533[695-1022] is a construct covering residues 695-1022. The clinically important resistance mutations (T790M, C797S) and the activating mutations (L858R, exon-19 deletions) each have their own data.

One more caution: without an organism filter, the search also returns mouse Egfr (Q01279, 86 measurements). Add "human" (organism='Homo sapiens') when you mean the human protein.

The lesson. Decide which target you mean before you ask for ligands. Use the wild-type id (520) for the classic "EGFR inhibitor" question. Use a mutant id for resistance questions. Use the UniProt accession with variants included when you want everything measured on the gene.

How this was answered

  1. search_targets with query "EGFR" and organism='Homo sapiens': matches gene names, protein names, UniProt entry names and accessions. Exact matches and the targets with the most data rank first.
  2. get_target with id 520: identifiers, synonyms, PDB ids and counts by affinity type.
  3. run_sql: counted all targets that share UniProt P00533 and their measurements.
